# Jojo Usafi Project Constitution

Jojo Usafi is a scalable multi-brand ecommerce retailer.

Initial market:
Tanzania.

Currency:
TZS.

Languages:
English and Kiswahili.

EcoPlus is the initial catalogue only.

## Non-negotiable principles

1. Mobile first.
2. Very simple admin dashboard.
3. Supabase PostgreSQL is the operational database. Firebase is permanently unapproved.
4. Google Sheets is the human-friendly catalogue control layer.
5. Catalogue synchronization works both directions, and is validated.
6. SKU is a stable product identifier.
7. Orders are recorded before WhatsApp.
8. Important actions are auditable.
9. Ecommerce actions are measurable.
10. Future brands must not require rebuilding the application.
11. Security and data integrity take priority.
12. The EcoPlus live website is the canonical storefront visual reference.
13. Nothing is invented. Prices, stock, descriptions and product identity come from the
    Product Master, or they do not appear at all. Suspicious data is reported to Ibrahim,
    never silently corrected.
14. A product with no approved photograph is not shown to customers.
